================================================================================
TASK 11: PROFIT MARGIN AND REGIONAL ANALYSIS IN TABLEAU
Dataset: Sample - Superstore (or Superstore.xls / .csv)
Operations: Calculated Field (Profit Margin) -> Regional Aggregations -> Bar Chart -> Color Encoding -> Business Insights
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: OPEN DATASET IN TABLEAU
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Launch Tableau Desktop.
2. Under "Connect" on the left panel:
   - If using the built-in sample, click "Sample - Superstore" under "Saved Data Sources".
   - Or click "Microsoft Excel" / "Text file", browse to your Superstore file, and open it.
3. Drag the "Orders" table into the Canvas area if not automatically loaded.
4. Click on "Sheet 1" at the bottom to open the worksheet interface.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: CREATE CALCULATED FIELD: "PROFIT MARGIN"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the "Data" pane on the far left, click the small dropdown arrow next to the 
   search bar, OR right-click any blank area in the Data pane.
2. Select "Create Calculated Field...".
3. In the calculation editor dialog:
   - Name the field at the top: Profit Margin (%)
   - Enter the exact formula:
     (SUM([Profit]) / SUM([Sales])) * 100
     *(Note: Using SUM([Profit]) / SUM([Sales]) is essential in Tableau so that it calculates 
     the true weighted margin at the aggregated regional level, rather than averaging raw row ratios).*
4. Verify that the bottom says: "The calculation is valid."
5. Click "OK".
6. Format as Percentage/Number:
   - In the Data pane, right-click on the new "Profit Margin (%)" measure.
   - Select "Default Properties" -> "Number Format...".
   - Select "Number (Custom)" -> Decimal places: 2 -> Suffix: "%" (or select Percentage).
   - Click "OK".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: BUILD THE BAR CHART FOR REGIONAL SALES & AVERAGE PROFIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Place Dimensions:
   - From the Data pane, drag "Region" to the **Rows** shelf.
     (You will see 4 rows: Central, East, South, West).
2. Place Measures for Total Sales and Average Profit:
   - Drag "Sales" to the **Columns** shelf. (It will default to `SUM(Sales)`).
   - Drag "Profit" to the **Columns** shelf next to `SUM(Sales)`.
   - By default, it will be `SUM(Profit)`.
   - To change it to Average Profit as requested:
     * Right-click on the `SUM(Profit)` pill on the Columns shelf.
     * Go to "Measure (Sum)" -> select **"Average"**.
     * The pill now displays as `AVG(Profit)`.
3. You now have two side-by-side horizontal bar charts comparing Total Sales and Average Profit per Region.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: APPLY COLOR ENCODING TO HIGHLIGHT PROFIT MARGIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Locate the **Marks** card (to the left of the chart area).
2. Notice the tabs under Marks: "All", "SUM(Sales)", and "AVG(Profit)".
3. Click on the **All** tab (or the specific measure tab you want to color).
4. Drag your calculated field **`Profit Margin (%)`** from the Data pane and drop it onto the **Color** tile on the Marks card.
5. Customize Color Palette:
   - In the Color Legend appearing on the right, click the small dropdown arrow -> select "Edit Colors...".
   - In the Palette dropdown, select **"Red-Green Diverging"** (or "Orange-Blue Diverging").
   - Check the box: "Include Totals" (if totals are on) or "Stepped Color" (e.g., 5 steps).
   - Click "Advanced >>":
     * Set "Center" to: 0 (or set to average margin ~ 12%).
     * Setting Center to 0 ensures any negative/loss-making margins show bright red, 
       while profitable margins show green.
   - Click "Apply" and "OK".
6. Add Data Labels:
   - Click on the **Label** tile on the Marks card -> check "Show mark labels".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: INSIGHTS ON REGIONAL PROFIT MARGIN VARIATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observe the generated visual:
1. West Region:
   - Highest Profit Margin (~14.8% - 15.2%).
   - High Total Sales combined with the highest Average Profit per order.
   - Indicates healthy pricing power, lower shipping costs, and strong product mix.

2. East Region:
   - Strong second place (~13.5% - 14.0% margin) with consistent total sales volume.

3. South Region:
   - Moderate Profit Margin (~11.9%). Lower overall sales volume compared to East and West.

4. Central Region:
   - Lowest Profit Margin (~7.9% - 8.5%).
   - Despite generating substantial Total Sales, the profit margin is severely depressed.
   - Root Cause Analysis: The Central region suffers from aggressive discounting 
     (e.g., in Texas, Illinois, and Ohio) which erodes margins.

Business Strategic Recommendations:
- Restructure discount policies in the Central region to stem profit leakage.
- Replicate promotional strategies and inventory mix from the West region across Central branches.
