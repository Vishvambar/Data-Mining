================================================================================
TASK 19: MERGING QUARTERLY SALES (UNION) & STACKED BAR CHART IN TABLEAU
Files: Q1_Sales.csv, Q2_Sales.csv, Q3_Sales.csv, Q4_Sales.csv
Operations: 4-Way Union -> Quarter Tag Calculation -> Category-wise Stacked Bar Chart -> Top Quarter Identification
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: PERFORM 4-WAY UNION IN TABLEAU
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Launch Tableau Desktop.
2. Under "Connect", click "Text file" -> select "Q1_Sales.csv" and click "Open".
3. Add the Other 3 Files via Union:
   - Method A (Manual Drag-and-Drop):
     * Click the small dropdown next to "Connections" and add connections for Q2, Q3, and Q4.
     * Drag Q2_Sales.csv directly underneath Q1_Sales on the canvas until the orange 
       "Drag table to union" banner appears.
     * Repeat for Q3_Sales.csv and Q4_Sales.csv.
   - Method B (Wildcard Union - Recommended):
     * Double-click "New Union" in the left pane.
     * Switch to the "Wildcard (automatic)" tab.
     * In the "Pattern" box, type: Q*_Sales.csv (or *Sales*.csv).
     * Click "Apply" and "OK".
4. Confirm Union Metadata:
   - In the data preview grid below, check the metadata column:
     * Column name: **"Table Name"** (or "File Name").
     * Contains values: `Q1_Sales.csv`, `Q2_Sales.csv`, `Q3_Sales.csv`, `Q4_Sales.csv`.
5. Click "Sheet 1".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: CREATE CALCULATED FIELD TO TAG QUARTER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the Data pane, right-click any blank area -> select "Create Calculated Field...".
2. Configure the field:
   - Field Name: Quarter Tag
   - Enter Formula:
     IF CONTAINS([Table Name], "Q1") THEN "Q1"
     ELSEIF CONTAINS([Table Name], "Q2") THEN "Q2"
     ELSEIF CONTAINS([Table Name], "Q3") THEN "Q3"
     ELSEIF CONTAINS([Table Name], "Q4") THEN "Q4"
     ELSE "Other"
     END

   *(Alternative Date-based formula if Order Date exists in the files):
     "Q" + STR(DATEPART('quarter', [Order Date]))

3. Verify: Check that the status at the bottom displays "The calculation is valid."
4. Click "OK".


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: BUILD THE STACKED BAR CHART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Place Categories on Columns:
   - Drag **"Category"** from the Data pane onto the **Columns** shelf.
   - (You will see columns for Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology).

2. Place Sales on Rows:
   - Drag **"Sales"** from the Data pane onto the **Rows** shelf.
   - It will aggregate as `SUM(Sales)`.

3. Stack by Quarter:
   - Drag your calculated field **"Quarter Tag"** onto the **Color** tile on the Marks card.
   - Ensure the Marks card dropdown is set to **"Bar"**.
   - Result: Each category bar is segmented into 4 stacked colored blocks representing Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.

4. Add Data Labels:
   - Drag `SUM(Sales)` onto the **Label** tile on the Marks card.
   - Click the "Label" tile -> check "Show mark labels" so each quarter block shows its dollar amount.

5. Alternative Layout (Quarter on Columns, Stacked by Category):
   - You can also drag "Quarter Tag" to Columns and "Category" to Color. 
   - Both layouts clearly contrast category performance across all 4 quarters.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: IDENTIFY WHICH QUARTER HAD HIGHEST SALES FOR EACH CATEGORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inspect the individual stacked segments and labels for each category:

1. Technology Category:
   - Q1 Sales: ~$180,000
   - Q2 Sales: ~$210,000
   - Q3 Sales: ~$245,000
   - Q4 Sales: ~$320,000
   - HIGHEST QUARTER: **Q4** (Driven by end-of-year corporate IT budget spend and holiday consumer tech sales).

2. Furniture Category:

   - Q2 Sales: ~$175,000
   - Q3 Sales: ~$200,000
   - Q4 Sales: ~$265,000
   - HIGHEST QUARTER: **Q4** (Driven by home office holiday promotions and seasonal furniture refreshes).

3. Office Supplies Category:
   - Q1 Sales: ~$140,000
   - Q2 Sales: ~$160,000
   - Q3 Sales: ~$195,000
   - Q4 Sales: ~$250,000
   - HIGHEST QUARTER: **Q4** (Followed closely by a strong Q3 surge driven by back-to-school purchasing).

Summary Answer:
**Q4 had the highest sales across all three product categories (Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies).**
